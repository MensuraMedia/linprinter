Step 2: try another USB cable

A cable is the cheapest thing to rule out, and the most common cause. On this printer, two faulty cables caused every connection failure for a week, including one that worked for a few minutes before failing.

  1. Use a short USB 2.0 printer cable (A to B), under 2 m.
  2. Plug it straight into the computer, not into a hub or extension.
  3. Wait a few seconds. LinPrinter notices the printer by itself.

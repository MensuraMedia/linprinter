## Lessons
- GTK 3: `show_all` skips `no_show_all` widgets, so show a hidden row's children before hiding it.
- Wrapping labels need `max_width_chars`, or cards in a FlowBox claim the full text width.
- A status the app hasn't received yet is "checking", never "ok".
